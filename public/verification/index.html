What “verified” means

A field is verified against the named source. “Not disclosed” means the checked standard metadata and obvious model-card sections did not declare the value; it does not prove the information exists nowhere else.

Runtime support

Runtime entries are source-derived. They are not claims that Open Model Weights executed the model on that runtime.
