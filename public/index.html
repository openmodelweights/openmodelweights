FAQ

What does “open-weight” mean?

Is an open-weight model automatically open source?

No. “Open-weight” means model weight files are available under some set of terms. Source code, training data, training recipes and evaluation assets may have different availability.

Does Open Model Weights certify a model's license?

No. We record source-backed metadata and link to authoritative terms. License summaries are informational and are not legal advice.

Why publish the registry as JSON?

So developers, agents, researchers and other directories can consume the same structured records that power the website.
