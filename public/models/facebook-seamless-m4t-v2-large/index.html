What is seamless-m4t-v2-large?

OMW evidence review3 Oct 2026

Open Model Weights records seamless-m4t-v2-large as a 2.30925B-parameter audio → text open-weight model in the seamless-m4t-v2-large family, with Meta listed as the developer.
